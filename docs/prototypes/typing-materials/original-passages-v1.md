# 打字练习原创样文 V1

适用对象：七、八年级。中英文各 6 篇，每种语言 3 个拟定难度，每档 2 篇。这些档位依据用字、句式和标点设计，尚未用学生试打数据校准。英文正文统一使用键盘可直接输入的半角标点。

## 中文

### zh-01 · 基础 · 早到十分钟

小路今天比平时早到学校十分钟。走廊还很安静，教室里只有窗边的一束光。他把书包放好，先擦黑板，再把窗子推开一条缝。风吹过来，桌上的一张纸轻轻动了一下。

同桌进门时，问他为什么来得这么早。小路说，他想试一试不用一路小跑是什么感觉。两个人一起去打水，回到座位后，又读了几页书。铃声终于响了，小路低头看了一眼课表。原来十分钟没有很长，却足够让早晨慢一点。他把这件事记在本子上，准备明天再试一次。

### zh-02 · 基础 · 归还一把伞

下午放学时下起了雨。小雨站在校门口，摸了摸书包，发现自己忘了带伞。值班老师从门边拿起一把蓝伞，告诉她用完以后可以放回原处。小雨撑开伞，慢慢走到车站。

第二天早上，地面还没干。小雨把伞晾好，带回了学校。她看到门边已经多了几把伞，有长的，也有短的。她把蓝伞放回架子上，将把手朝外，方便下一个人拿取。到了下午，又有人在门边停下来，伸手拿走了那把伞。小雨从窗边看见了，笑着收起自己的书。

### zh-03 · 进阶 · 树影走了多远

午休后，科学小组在操场边选了一棵树。他们想知道，树影在一节课里会走多远。小组长用粉笔在影子的末端画了一个小圈，另一位同学记下时间。大家约定，每隔十分钟回来做一次标记。

第一次回去时，影子已经移出了小圈。第二次，云挡住了太阳，地上的边界变得模糊。有人提议先估一个位置，记录员却把这一栏留空，并写下“有云”。最后，他们用线把看得清的几个点连起来。老师请他们保留那个空格，因为一次诚实的记录，比一条看起来完整的线更有用。

### zh-04 · 进阶 · 黑板上的两条路线

班级准备为校园开放日做一张路线图。教学楼到展厅可以走中间的台阶，也可以沿右侧的缓坡绕过去。有人想把两条路线都画成红色，有人觉得这样会让第一次来的人分不清。

小组把地图贴到门上，请一位没见过草稿的同学试着找路。那位同学站在门口看了一会儿，竟然把箭头读反了。小组重新标出入口，把路线分成实线与虚线，再在转弯处写上能看见的建筑物。第二次试走顺利多了。他们这才发现，一张地图画完以后，还需要有人用过才算真的完成。

### zh-05 · 挑战 · 记录里的误差

午后的阵雨停了，社团把两个量杯摆在不同的位置，打算比较一段时间内接到的雨水。回收时，他们才发现靠窗的量杯立在斜坡上，刻度虽然看得清，读数却可能偏高。另一只量杯边缘还有几颗没有滑下去的水珠。

有人主张删掉这组数据，也有人坚持把它和其他读数一起计算。负责记录的同学在表格旁补了一列“测量条件”，逐项写下杯口遮挡、摆放角度和读取时间，再把这组结果单独讨论。真正需要解释的，不只是两只杯子差了多少，而是这种差异究竟来自雨水，还是来自他们自己的测量方式。

### zh-06 · 挑战 · 给旧地图留一角

整理资料柜时，值日生找到一张折得发白的校园地图。图上的小径穿过一片空地，而那里现在已经建起实验楼。有人建议把旧图扔掉，免得参观的人走错；还有人想把它当成当前地图的底稿，省去重新绘制的时间。

指导老师没有立刻答应。他取来两张透明纸，让同学分别描下原来的道路和现在的道路。线条重叠的地方讲得清楚，改变的地方更值得留意。展板完成后，旧图被装进左下角，旁边注明了年份。来参观的人可以先看今天的路线，再想一想，没有实验楼的时候，校园里的脚步会往哪里去。

## English

### en-01 · 基础 · The Blue Folder

Ben came into the classroom before the bell. He put his blue folder on the desk and looked inside. The worksheet was there, but his pencil was missing. He checked under the desk and in the front pocket of his bag. At last, he found the pencil between two books.

His friend Mia came in and asked if he was ready for class. Ben said he was ready now. They opened their workbooks and read the first question together. It asked them to draw a path from the door to the school garden. Ben drew the path past the library. Mia took a shorter path past the hall. When the teacher came in, both students held up their maps. The teacher smiled and asked them to walk each path after class. They wanted to see which way was really shorter.

### en-02 · 基础 · A Seat by the Window

Nina liked to sit by the window on the bus. She could see the old bridge, the small bakery, and a row of trees near the river. One morning, the window seat was taken. An older woman was sitting there with a large bag on her knees, so Nina sat across the aisle.

At the next stop, a boy got on with a box. The bus began to move before he found a seat. Nina slid over and made room for him. He put the box down and opened the lid a little. Inside were paper boats for a school project. Nina helped him hold the box when the bus turned. When they reached school, he gave her a small green boat. She had missed the view, but she had found something new to bring to class.

### en-03 · 进阶 · The Missing Label

In the science room, four cups stood in a neat row. Each held a bean seed, some soil, and a little water. The group wanted to compare plants kept in light and shade. They had written a label for every cup, but one label came loose when they moved the tray.

Adam was sure the unlabeled cup had been near the window. Leila pointed to a photograph from the day before. It showed the whole tray, though the corner was hard to read. They compared a mark on the cup with the mark in the picture and agreed on its place. Then they wrote the cup number directly on the side with a pencil. The group still checked its results with care. A neat table only tells a useful story when each number belongs to the correct thing.

### en-04 · 进阶 · The Longer Way

Two groups walked from the school gate to the sports field. One group went through a narrow passage behind the building. The other followed a wider path around the garden. At first, most students expected the narrow passage to be quicker. It looked shorter on the school map.

The first group had to stop twice when other students came the opposite way. On the garden path, the second group could keep moving without a break. Both groups reached the field at nearly the same time. Back in class, they marked the stops on their maps and discussed what the clock had measured. Distance was one part of the answer. The width of a path, the number of people, and the places where everyone had to wait were also part of the journey.

### en-05 · 挑战 · A Result Worth Checking

When the class tested paper bridges, one of the weakest designs held more weight than anyone expected. The students cheered, but Farah noticed something unusual. The bridge had shifted while they were adding washers, and a paper edge was touching the table. Some of the weight might have been supported by the table rather than the bridge.

The group wrote down exactly what happened and tested the same design again. This time, they marked the positions of the supports and checked the space below the bridge before placing each washer. The second result was lower. They kept both results in their notes and drew a small sketch to show the difference. During the report, Farah explained why they had repeated the test. The surprising number had helped them improve the way they measured, even though it did not describe the design fairly.

### en-06 · 挑战 · Copies of a Changing Map

The library received two maps of the same neighborhood. One had been drawn before the railway opened, while the other showed a station where a farm had once stood. Arun wanted to display the newer map because it would be more useful to visitors. Chloe argued that the older map could help people understand why some streets ended suddenly.

They laid a sheet of tracing paper over the maps and marked a few buildings that appeared on both. Next, they traced the former farm road and the present railway in different colors. Several streets followed parts of the old road; others turned toward the station. The display eventually used both maps, with their dates and a short set of questions. A visitor could find the library on the new map and then look back to see how the route had changed.
